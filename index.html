Board settings

Scientific pitch, including octave. Standard guitar tuning is E2 A2 D3 G3 B3 E4. Changing tuning changes the pitch receipt, while fret positions stay fixed.

THE SELECTED SHAPE

Edit voicing

Six space-separated entries. x = muted, 0 = open, 1–24 = fret. Fretted notes must fit a five-fret window.

Use - for no label, or 1–4 on a fretted string. Repeating a finger label can record a barre; no connecting barre line is drawn.

A label is your note, not an automatically verified chord name.
